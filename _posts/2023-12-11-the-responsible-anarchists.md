We’re responsibile anarchists. We don’t abide by the status quo. We don’t work from anywhere, we work *anywhere*. Our work is not *agile*, is fast. We’re optimists and pessimists. We like to compete, but *adore* to fight. We don’t develop explorers, we craft them. Our only law is code, and our only future is onchain.
