I'm Giacomo, usually Jaack online. I'm an independent builder and operator working across crypto, AI, and native apps.

I like shipping, spotting markets early, and making complicated things usable. Writing is how I figure out what I actually think.

How I got here

I started Tweaknology with university friends in 2014. That led to tech journalism for Webnews, La Stampa, and Saggiamente, and community work with Campus Party. Explaining technical subjects taught me to start with the person reading.

I joined Avascan in 2020 and later became Head of Growth at Routescan, working on partnerships and explorer infrastructure. I left in May 2026. It was an important chapter; my work now is independent.

How I work

I like a concrete problem and a small enough scope to understand the details. Writing helps me test an idea before committing more time to it. I’m interested in the point where technical choices become useful to someone else.

I use OpenClaw and specialist agents to help coordinate the work. The part I care about is checking outcomes and keeping the decisions understandable. I want more room to build, think, and enjoy life.

The portfolio shows what I’m building. Now is where I keep current priorities.

Ecosystem, advisory, and speaking

As Italy Coordinator for Team1, I connect local organizers and builders around Avalanche through workshops, events, and educational material. My Redbridge advisory work focuses on token design and the ecosystem around privacy-preserving cross-chain infrastructure.

I also study Avalanche Community Proposals and turn the mechanics into research and interactive tools.
