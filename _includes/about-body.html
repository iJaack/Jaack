I'm Giacomo, usually Jaack online. I work on growth, build products, and write to understand what I'm learning.

I lead growth at Routescan, working on partnerships and the adoption of blockchain explorer infrastructure. Alongside that, I build native apps, research tools, and systems for coordinating work with AI agents.

The connection is practical: I like taking something complex, finding out where it's useful, and making it easier for someone else to use.

How I got here

I started Tweaknology with university friends in 2014. That led to tech journalism for Webnews, La Stampa, and Saggiamente, and community work with Campus Party. Explaining AI, blockchain, and other technical subjects taught me to start with the person reading, not the technology.

I joined Avascan in 2020 and continued with Routescan as the work expanded beyond Avalanche. Growth became a way to connect product decisions, infrastructure, and the people building on it.

What I'm building

My projects include Sea Temperature, THICC, Gem Mint Strategy, and Eva. Behind them is a Portfolio OS I use to connect project context, decisions, and AI-assisted workflows. I care about useful output, clear limits, and checking what actually happened.
