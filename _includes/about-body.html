I'm Giacomo, usually Jaack online. I'm an independent builder and operator working across crypto, AI, and native apps.

My work today centers on Eva, Gem Mint Strategy, and THICC, alongside a small portfolio of Apple-platform apps. I also coordinate Team1 Italy and advise Redbridge.

I like shipping, spotting markets early, and making complicated things usable. Writing is how I figure out what I actually think.

How I got here

I started Tweaknology with university friends in 2014. That led to tech journalism for Webnews, La Stampa, and Saggiamente, and community work with Campus Party. Explaining technical subjects taught me to start with the person reading.

I joined Avascan in 2020 and later became Head of Growth at Routescan, working on partnerships and explorer infrastructure. I left in May 2026. It was an important chapter; my work now is independent.

What I'm building

Eva spans AI identity and distribution. Within it, Eva Protocol is a product for public prediction theses, evidence, and reputation. Gem Mint Strategy brings my interest in Pokémon cards into an onchain portfolio. THICC looks at what a token's liquidity can actually support.

The apps solve smaller, everyday problems: sea-temperature maps, bodyweight training, focus sessions, and shipment tracking. I keep their scope focused enough to make the details matter.

Behind the projects is my Portfolio OS, built around OpenClaw and a set of specialist agents. It connects context, decisions, and follow-through. The point is to get more done while still enjoying life, with clear limits and checks on what the agents actually did.

Cosa sto costruendo

Eva comprende il lavoro su identità AI e distribuzione. Al suo interno, Eva Protocol è un prodotto per tesi previsionali pubbliche, evidenze e reputazione. Gem Mint Strategy porta il mio interesse per le carte Pokémon in un portafoglio onchain. THICC studia cosa possa sostenere davvero la liquidità di un token.

Le app risolvono problemi più piccoli e quotidiani: mappe della temperatura del mare, allenamento a corpo libero, sessioni di concentrazione e tracking delle spedizioni. Ne mantengo il perimetro abbastanza contenuto da poter curare i dettagli.

Dietro ai progetti c'è il mio Portfolio OS, costruito intorno a OpenClaw e a un gruppo di agenti specializzati. Collega contesto, decisioni ed esecuzione. L'obiettivo è fare di più continuando a godermi la vita, con limiti chiari e verifiche su ciò che gli agenti hanno fatto davvero.

Esplora i progetti e il mio ruolo, oppure scopri a cosa sto dedicando tempo.
