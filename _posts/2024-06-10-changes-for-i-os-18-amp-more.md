- **Gentler Streak** is being sherlocked, so I can cancel the subscription
- **Training Peaks** is being partially sherlocked (I won’t have workout planning, but I wasn’t using it very good, so I don’t mind)
- Evaluate to remove **Day One** in favor of Journal (tagging system is not present on iOS 17, maybe it will on iOS 18?)
- Buy new iPhone because my iPhone 14 Pro Max doesn’t support **Apple Intelligence**
- Buy new **iPad Pro 11”** because mine doesn’t support Apple Intelligence (+ Apple Pencil Pro + Magic Keyboard)
- Still doesn’t make sense to upgrade to an iPad-only setup, (so no 13”) as the new macOS enables **iPhone mirroring** that’s super useful a lot of times, especially when traveling
- When leasing of the MacBook Pro ends in 2025, it’ll most probably make sense to buy the **MacBook Air 15”** to take advantage of the bigger screen - I don’t need the capabilities of the Pro models anymore
- **1Password** is likely being shelocked completely, so cancel that subscription as well
- Still need **AllTrails** subscription for a year, as new Maps hiking routes are only available in *U.S. and Japan*
- I’ll probably need the **AirPods** **Pro** **2nd** **generation** if I want the **Voice Isolation** feature, that’s pretty helpful when I talk on the phone in loud environments (often when I’m on business trips)
